SOURCE: Yelp reviews — Grand Avenue Apartments, Grand Ave, St. Paul MN
DATE: Collected for UST Unofficial Housing Guide

---

Review by Natalie S. | ★★★★☆ (4/5)
"Solid choice for UST students, responsive management"
Been here for two years. Management (Sievert Properties) actually picks up the phone and responds to emails within a day or two. Maintenance for an urgent issue (burst pipe under my sink) came within 4 hours — I was impressed. Units are older and the finishes aren't as fancy as Summit Flats, but everything works. Heating has been reliable both winters. Rent is $995/month for my 1BR, which feels fair for the location. One parking spot included in my lease — huge bonus.

---

Review by Tyler W. | ★★★☆☆ (3/5)
"Average apartment, great street"
The apartment itself is nothing special — older windows that let in some draft, carpet that's seen better days, small kitchen. But the location on Grand Ave is excellent. Coffee shops, restaurants, bars, and a grocery store all within walking distance. The walk to UST campus takes me 12 minutes. If you care more about neighborhood access than unit quality, this is your place. Rent was $960 for 1BR when I moved in.

---

Review by Camille D. | ★★★★★ (5/5)
"Best apartment experience I've had in the Cities"
I've rented in three St. Paul buildings and this is by far the best. Maintenance comes when scheduled. The manager (I won't name her but she's been there 5 years) actually knows the tenants and genuinely cares. I've never had an issue go unresolved for more than a week. The building is older but well-maintained. No mold, no pest issues, no heating drama. $1,050/month for a 1BR with parking included. I've referred two friends and they've both been happy.

---

Review by Marcus B. | ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)
"Noise from Grand Ave is real"
The location is great for everything except peace and quiet. Grand Ave has a lot of foot traffic, car traffic, and bar noise on weekends. My unit faces the street and I regularly hear music until 1–2am on Friday and Saturday nights. If you're a light sleeper or study late at home, ask for a unit facing the back. The street-facing units are meaningfully noisier. Management was fine for me — responsive, no complaints there. Just the noise issue.

---

Review by Jasmine L. | ★★★★☆ (4/5)
"Good value, especially if you're a UST student"
The building has a lot of UST students which makes it feel like a community. Neighbors are quiet during the week (everyone has class/work). One thing I didn't expect: the building has a small study lounge in the basement that's actually great for focused work. It's not advertised. Ask about it. Heat is included in rent which matters A LOT in Minnesota — my previous place charged separately and my January bill was $180. Here I pay one flat rate. Worth factoring that into price comparisons.
