Review by Sarah K. | ★★★☆☆ (3/5)
"Beautiful building, frustrating management"
Lived here for 14 months. The building itself is stunning — exposed brick, hardwood floors, really nice common areas. But maintenance requests go into a black hole. My oven burner stopped working and I waited 3 weeks for a repair. When I followed up by email I got a response within a day, so I learned you MUST follow up in writing. Location is unbeatable if you go to UST. Would consider coming back if management improves.

---

Review by James T. | ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)
"Mold in the bathroom — documented, never fully fixed"
Moved in August 2023. By October I had visible black mold growing in the grout of my shower and on the ceiling near the exhaust fan. Submitted a maintenance request with photos. They came, bleached it, and painted over it. By December it was back. I submitted again. This time they replaced a small section of drywall. The mold came back a third time. I eventually broke my lease citing habitability (had a lawyer friend advise me) and got most of my deposit back after disputing. If you're considering this building, check the bathroom exhaust fans on your tour — if they're not strong, moisture builds up.

---

Review by Priya M. | ★★★★☆ (4/5)
"Great location, acceptable apartment"
I've lived here two years. I know it has mixed reviews but honestly my experience has been fine. Maintenance takes 5-10 days for non-urgent stuff, which isn't ideal but not outrageous. My unit is on the 3rd floor facing the courtyard so it's quiet. Heating works fine — I think the problems others describe are in the older west wing. The walk to UST is genuinely great, and Grand Ave being 5 minutes away makes weekend life really easy. Parking is $75/month extra and worth it in winter.

---

Review by Derek L. | ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)
"Thin walls, noisy neighbors, slow responses"
The insulation in this building is basically nonexistent. I can hear my upstairs neighbor's alarm clock. I can hear conversations from the hallway clearly. Management response to the noise complaint was "we'll note it on file" — nothing was done. Also the laundry in my wing broke for two weeks in January and I had to take laundry elsewhere. Not what I expected for $1,150/month.

---

Review by Megan R. | ★★★★★ (5/5)
"Love it here — maybe got lucky with my unit"
I've had zero maintenance issues in 18 months. My unit is in the east wing, second floor, and it's warm, quiet, and the in-unit laundry is a lifesaver. Management has been responsive to my two requests (a leaky faucet — fixed in 3 days, and a window latch — fixed in 5 days). Maybe I got lucky, but this is the best apartment I've had in St. Paul. The rent is on the higher end ($1,180 for a 1BR) but I feel like I'm getting what I pay for.

---

Review by Alex C. | ★★★☆☆ (3/5)
"Fine if you know what you're getting into"
Coming in with realistic expectations: this is a mid-tier apartment with an A-tier location. Management is slow, walls are thin, parking is extra. But you're on Summit Ave, you can walk to campus, and Grand Ave is right there. If location is your top priority and you have a car-free lifestyle, it makes sense. If you need fast maintenance or need to park a car cheaply, look elsewhere.
