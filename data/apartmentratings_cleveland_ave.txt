SOURCE: ApartmentRatings.com — Cleveland Avenue rental buildings, St. Paul MN (near UST)
DATE: Collected for UST Unofficial Housing Guide

Property: 1840 Cleveland Ave S — Rating: 2.8/5

Review by verified tenant:
"Cheap for a reason. The price ($775/month for a 1BR) is genuinely low for the area, and the location is fine — takes about 20 minutes to walk to UST. But the building shows its age. My unit had an ongoing water stain on the ceiling from the unit above me. The landlord patched it twice and it came back. The basement laundry machines are old and two of the four dryers don't dry fully. Heat is included which is good. The neighbors are a mixed bag — mostly quiet but there's one unit that plays bass-heavy music until midnight on weekdays. If you're on a tight budget and can tolerate the trade-offs, it works."

---

Review by verified tenant:
"I found mold under my bathroom sink when I moved out. It was behind the cabinet panel and I missed it on move-in inspection. I wish I had pulled the cabinet doors open fully during tour. The maintenance guy (one person handles the whole building) is friendly but overwhelmed — he's trying to manage 30 units alone. Response times are measured in weeks not days. That said, rent is low, location is walkable to UST, and I never had a pest problem, which I've heard is an issue in other buildings on this block."

---

Property: 1920 Cleveland Ave S — Rating: 3.4/5

Review by verified tenant:
"Better managed than the other Cleveland Ave buildings I've seen. The property manager lives on-site, which makes a real difference — if something breaks, I can knock on his door. My unit is a small 1BR at $825/month, heat included. Windows are old and drafty in the worst cold snaps, but nothing severe. The on-site parking is $50/month extra, which is cheaper than most alternatives in the area. I've been here 2 years and would renew except I'm graduating."

---

Review by verified tenant:
"On-site manager is great and keeps the building clean and pest-free. My only real complaints are small: the kitchen is tiny (apartment probably built in the 1960s) and there's no in-unit laundry — shared laundry in the basement that's adequate but not great. For $825 in Mac-Groveland within walking distance of UST, I genuinely can't complain about much. This is the hidden gem of Cleveland Ave."

---

Property: 2050 Cleveland Ave S — Rating: 2.1/5

Review by verified tenant:
"Do not rent here. I'm writing this because I wish someone had warned me. The landlord (individual, not a management company) is responsive before you sign and then basically disappears. I had a broken radiator for two weeks in February — I slept in my coat. He eventually sent someone but only after I sent a certified letter referencing Minnesota tenant law on habitable conditions. The building also had a mouse problem in late fall that took three months to address. I broke my lease at month 8 and lost my deposit. Would not return."

---

Review by verified tenant:
"The rent is rock bottom ($710/month) and the location is okay but you get what you pay for. The common areas are neglected — hallway hasn't been painted since maybe 2005, mailboxes are rusty. My unit itself was fine — no major issues in my year there. I think it depends on your specific unit. Ground floor and basement units have the worst issues; upper floors seem better maintained. I was on the 2nd floor and didn't have the problems I heard downstairs tenants complaining about."
