From u/FoodieAtUST:
"Grand Ave is genuinely one of the best streets in the Twin Cities for eating out. Here are the spots that students I know love:
- Cossetta Alimentari: Classic Italian-American, great for a nicer dinner. The bakery side has great lunch options.
- Moscow on the Hill: Russian food, fun atmosphere, strong drinks — popular for celebrations.
- Cafe Latte: The line is always long but the soup and sandwiches are worth it. Great for studying.
- Wapasa Coffee: Small independent coffee shop, better than any chain, great for studying."

---

From u/GroceryGuide_UST:
"Grocery options near campus:
- Kowalski's on Grand Ave (about 12 min walk from most Mac-Groveland apartments): Nice but expensive. Good for specialty items, not great for budget shopping.
- Lunds & Byerlys in Highland Park: Higher-end, same issue.
- Aldi on West 7th (bus or bike): Cheapest option in the area. Worth the extra trip.
- Target on University Ave (Route 21 bus): Great for household supplies plus a decent grocery section.
- Cub Foods in Highland: Midrange prices, good selection. Route 84 bus gets you there."

---

From u/StudentBudget_Eats:
"How to eat near UST without spending a fortune:
1. Kowalski's deli counter has hot food that's not marked up as badly as their packaged goods.
2. Grand Ave has a handful of spots with $10-12 lunch specials that are genuinely filling.
3. The Friday fish fry culture in St. Paul is real — $12-15 for a full fish fry at a neighborhood bar.
4. Meal prep wins. The Aldi run once a week is 100% the move if you're on a tight budget."

---

From u/NeighborhoodAmenities:
"Other useful places to know about if you're living in Mac-Groveland near UST:
- CVS on Grand Ave: for pharmacy and convenience items
- Hamline-Midway library: 15-min bus ride, great study spot with better AC than campus sometimes
- Bde Maka Ska (formerly Lake Calhoun) and Lake Harriet: 20-min bike ride, essential for summer sanity
- St. Paul public libraries: Grand Ave branch is walkable, good for quiet study
- Planet Fitness on West 7th: Cheapest gym option ($10-15/month), not glamorous but functional"

From u/CoffeeShopStudy:
"The best coffee shops for studying near UST in descending order of Wi-Fi reliability and seat availability:
1. Wapasa Coffee: Best coffee, decent seating, can get crowded on weekends
2. Dunn Bros on Grand: Reliable Wi-Fi, plenty of seating, open late on weekdays
3. Caribou Coffee on Grand: Chain but consistent, best for when you just need to power through
4. Cafe Latte: Great food but loud, not ideal for studying
Avoid going to any of these on Sunday afternoon before a Monday deadline — they will all be packed with UST students."
