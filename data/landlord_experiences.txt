POST: Sievert Properties (manages Grand Avenue Apartments and other buildings in Mac-Groveland)

From u/SivertTenant2023:
"I've rented from Sievert Properties for two years. Maintenance takes 4-7 days for standard requests, which is acceptable but not fast. Urgent requests (I had a leak) were handled same-day. They have an online portal for submitting requests which creates a paper trail — I appreciate that. Lease renewal was straightforward, rent went up 4% year 2, which is annoying but not outrageous."

From u/GrandAveRenter:
"Sievert is fine. Not exceptional, not terrible. They send maintenance updates by email. Once they sent someone who didn't have the right part and had to come back two days later — that's the worst I can say. For property management near UST they're solidly in the top tier."

POST: Harbor Bay Management (manages several properties in the Mac-Groveland/Highland area)

From u/FormerHarborBayTenant:
"Harbor Bay was a nightmare. I submitted a maintenance request for a broken furnace in November. It took two weeks to get any response. When they finally came, the technician said the furnace needed a part that would take 5 more days to arrive. I was without heat in Minnesota in November for 2.5 weeks total. They refused to provide any rent credit or compensation. I reported them to St. Paul Neighborhood Housing Services. Would never rent from them again."

From u/HarborBayWarning:
"They're fine until something goes wrong. While everything works you'll never interact with them. But when I needed to break my lease due to a job change, they were impossible — wouldn't discuss a subletter, threatened to keep my full deposit plus 2 months rent. I had to get a housing advocate from Legal Aid involved to negotiate a reasonable exit. If you sign with Harbor Bay, read the lease break clause extremely carefully."

From u/NeutralHarborBayReview:
"I've heard bad things about Harbor Bay but my experience was uneventful. Never needed maintenance, renewed my lease once, moved out cleanly. I think it depends on the specific building and whether your unit has issues. But I'd still be cautious — a landlord who's impossible when things go wrong is a real risk."

POST: Individual landlord at 1920 Cleveland Ave S (on-site owner-manager)

From u/ClevelandAveHiddenGem:
"The landlord at 1920 Cleveland lives in the building. His name is Frank (I'm not posting his last name). This is the best situation because he cares about the building like it's his home, because it is. Maintenance is fast — I knocked on his door once because my sink was backing up and he was there within an hour. He knows every tenant by name. Rent is $825 for a 1BR which is below market for the area. He raises rent $25-40/year which is very reasonable. The building is older but clean and well-maintained."

POST: Individual landlord at 2050 Cleveland Ave S

From u/ClevelandAveAvoid:
"I've written about this elsewhere but I want it on record: the landlord at 2050 Cleveland is unreachable by phone and inconsistent by email. I had a mouse infestation — plural mice — starting in October. I reported it immediately with photos. First response came 10 days later. They sent an exterminator who put out traps but no sealing of entry points. The mice came back within a week. This cycle repeated for 3 months. I eventually paid out of pocket for a different pest control company and sent the landlord the bill — he refused to reimburse. I moved out at the end of my lease and will never recommend this building to anyone."

POST: Unnamed landlord on Fairmount Ave (positive experience)

From u/FairmountFan:
"My landlord on Fairmount is a small local company — they own 3 buildings in the neighborhood. Very responsive, professional, and proactive. Last spring they did a building inspection and reached out to tenants about fixing anything before it became a problem. Never experienced that before. They use an online portal that actually works. Rent is $980 for a 1BR, heat not included but building is well-insulated. I'd highly recommend looking on Fairmount for off-campus housing — it's quieter than Grand Ave and still very close to campus."
